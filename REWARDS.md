# Contributor Rewards

Community donations (via GitHub Sponsors) partially support contributors.
Commercial revenue is separate and is not part of this pool.

---

## Activation threshold

Contributor rewards are activated once monthly donations consistently exceed
**$500/month**, averaged over three consecutive months.

Below this threshold, all donations are allocated to the Project Reserve and
Infrastructure. This ensures project survival comes before contributor
distribution.

---

## Allocation (once threshold is met)

| Category | Percentage | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Project Reserve & Emergency Fund | 45% | Project continuity, legal, infrastructure emergencies |
| Contributor Reward Pool | 25% | Merit-based rewards for contributors |
| Infrastructure & Operations | 20% | CI, hosting, domains, signing, monitoring |
| Ecosystem Growth & Grants | 10% | Bug bounties, plugin grants, docs initiatives |

---

## How rewards are determined

Rewards are **discretionary** and based on:

- Quality and complexity of contribution
- Long-term ecosystem impact
- Review and maintenance work
- Documentation quality
- Issue triage and community support

Rewards are NOT based on commit count or PR volume. Contribution farming
is not rewarded.

---

## Legal clarification

Contributor rewards are discretionary community grants. They do not create:

- Employment or contractor relationships
- Equity or ownership rights
- Entitlement to commercial licensing revenue
- Guaranteed payment obligations

---

## Transparency

Once donations are active, monthly summaries will be published publicly,
including donation totals, infrastructure spending, and reward distributions
(with recipient consent).
