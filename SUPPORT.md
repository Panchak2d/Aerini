# Getting Support

## Questions and discussions

Use **GitHub Discussions** for:
- How-to questions
- Workflow design help
- Feature ideas and suggestions

---

## Bug reports

Open a **GitHub Issue** for:
- Reproducible bugs with steps to reproduce
- Incorrect behavior in a specific node
- Documentation errors

Do not open issues for general questions — use Discussions.

---

## Security vulnerabilities

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open public issues for vulnerabilities.

---

## Community support

Community support is best-effort. There are no SLA guarantees for open source users.

---

## Commercial support

Commercial license holders may contact us. See [CONTACT.md](CONTACT.md).
