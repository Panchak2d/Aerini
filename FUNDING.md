# Funding

## Commercial licensing

If you need to get out from under AGPL-3.0's copyleft obligation, a commercial license is the way to do that: proprietary modifications permitted, no source disclosure required, for one named product or service per license.

[View pricing and license terms](https://aerini.org/pricing)

Relevant if you are building a product on top of Aerini, deploying a modified version as a service, or distributing it to clients without disclosing changes.

For financially supporting the project without needing any of the above, see Patreon below — it doesn't remove the AGPL obligation, but it's the primary way to directly fund day-to-day development. See [Aerini Community & Development Model.md](Aerini%20Community%20%26%20Development%20Model.md) for how it fits into the rest of the project's model.

## Patreon

[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/Panchak2d)

GitHub Sponsors isn't currently usable for this project's maintainer, so Patreon is the active donation channel.

## What the money goes toward

Maintenance, bug fixes, new node types and integrations, documentation, and security reviews.

## Open source commitment

Every version of Aerini already released under AGPL-3.0 stays available under AGPL-3.0 — that grant can't be taken back. It's the maintainer's firm intention to keep releasing future versions under AGPL-3.0 as well, for as long as the project continues, regardless of commercial success.
