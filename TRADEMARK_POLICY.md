# Trademark Policy

> Draft. "Aerini" and its logo are not registered trademarks — this policy
> describes how the name is used today, not a legal right that's been
> formally registered anywhere. It follows common practice for unregistered
> open source project names. Not legal advice.

## The name and logo

"Aerini" and the Aerini logo identify this specific project and the
Maintainer's official builds, website, and communication channels. They are
not currently registered with any trademark office.

## What's fine

- Referring to Aerini by name to describe compatibility, integration, or
  discussion ("built for Aerini," "an Aerini plugin," "compatible with
  Aerini").
- Forking the project under AGPL-3.0 and giving the fork its own name.
- Screenshots, tutorials, and reviews that use the name and logo to
  accurately describe the actual project.

## What to avoid

- Using the name "Aerini" or the logo in a way that implies your fork,
  plugin, service, or product is the official project, is endorsed by it,
  or is maintained by the Maintainer, when it isn't.
- Using the name or logo as the primary branding for a competing commercial
  product built on Aerini's code.
- Modifying the logo and presenting the result as Aerini's own mark.

If your fork or project is clearly and prominently marked as unofficial
(e.g. "MyTool, a fork of Aerini" rather than just "Aerini"), that's the kind
of use this policy is fine with.
