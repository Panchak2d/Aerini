# CLA Plain-English Summary

> This is not a legal document. Read [CLA.md](CLA.md) for the binding terms.
> When in doubt, the CLA governs.

---

## What you keep

- Copyright ownership of your contribution
- The right to use your own code in other projects
- The right to independently license your own contributions
- All rights not explicitly granted below

---

## What you grant the project

- The right to distribute your contribution under AGPL-3.0 (open source)
- The right to distribute your contribution under a commercial license
- These grants are **non-exclusive** (you are not giving up your own rights)

---

## Why a CLA is required

Flowo uses dual licensing (AGPL + commercial). Without a CLA, the maintainer
cannot legally include your contribution in a commercial license without
risking legal fragmentation. The CLA makes commercial sustainability possible
while you keep ownership of your work.

---

## The AI training restriction

The CLA prohibits use of your contribution as training data for machine learning
models. This restriction binds:

- the project maintainer, and
- parties who receive this software **directly from this project** with explicit
  notice of the restriction

**Scope note:** Parties who receive the AGPL-licensed code through downstream
distribution are governed by AGPL-3.0 terms only. The training restriction is a
binding commitment for direct recipients and a statement of the project's values
— it is not a claim of universal enforcement across all possible downstream uses.
See [AI_POLICY.md](AI_POLICY.md) for the full policy.

---

## Acquisition protection

The CLA includes provisions that prevent the AGPL-licensed version of the
codebase from being re-licensed to a proprietary-only license in the event of
an acquisition. The open source version remains open.

---

## What this does NOT do

- Does not create employment, partnership, or contractor status
- Does not give the maintainer exclusive ownership of your work
- Does not prevent you from using your own code in other projects
- Does not entitle you to commercial licensing revenue
---

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I keep copyright over my contribution?**
Yes. You retain full copyright ownership. You are granting a license to use your
contribution, not transferring ownership.

**Can I reuse my own contribution in other projects?**
Yes. You retain all rights to your own code. Signing this CLA does not restrict
what you do with your own work outside this project.

**Does the maintainer own my code after I sign?**
No. The maintainer receives a license to use your contribution. Ownership stays
with you.

**Why is a CLA required if the project is open source?**
Flowo uses dual licensing: AGPL-3.0 for open source use and a commercial license for proprietary use. The CLA gives the maintainer the right to offer commercial licenses without needing each contributor's individual permission each time. See [docs/dual-licensing.md](docs/dual-licensing.md).

**Can my code be used to train AI models?**
Not by the maintainer or any party that has agreed to this restriction. It is a contractual obligation, not a universal one: downstream recipients of AGPL-licensed copies are governed by AGPL-3.0 only and are not bound by it.

**Will I receive any revenue from commercial licenses?**
No. The CLA does not entitle contributors to commercial revenue.

**What happens if the project is acquired?**
Any acquirer must continue providing an AGPL-licensed version of the project.
Contributors have direct enforcement rights against any acquirer who violates
this requirement. See Section 13 of the CLA for full details.

**I found a typo in the docs. Do I really need to sign a CLA?**
Yes, for all contributions. It is a one-time signature that covers all future contributions automatically. The CLA Assistant process takes under a minute.
