# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Flowo is a technical project. We expect direct, constructive, professional
interaction. The bar is: would you say this in a technical review at a competent
engineering organization? If yes, it belongs here. If not, it does not.

---

## Expected behavior

- Constructive feedback focused on code and ideas, not people
- Direct disagreement expressed clearly and without hostility
- Good faith assumption about others' intentions
- Helping newer contributors without condescension

---

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or targeted hostility
- Discrimination based on any personal characteristic
- Deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing private information without consent

---

## Enforcement

Report issues to [flowo.automations@proton.me](mailto:flowo.automations@proton.me). Reports are handled privately.

Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent contribution ban,
depending on severity and pattern of behavior.

The goal is to maintain a functional, respectful environment — not to pursue
punitive outcomes.
