# Dual Licensing

Flowo is available under two licenses for the same codebase: AGPL-3.0 for open source use, and a commercial license for proprietary use. A user or company picks whichever fits their situation.

---

## Why Flowo uses this model

AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. If you run a modified version of `flowo-server` as a network service for others, AGPL-3.0 requires you to publish your modifications under the same license. Many companies building products or internal tools cannot do that.

The commercial license removes that requirement. Companies that need to keep their modifications proprietary buy a commercial license and the AGPL copyleft obligation no longer applies.

MongoDB, Qt, MariaDB, and GitLab all use the same model. It is a common approach for open source projects that need a revenue path without abandoning open source.

---

## Why the CLA is required

Copyright belongs to whoever wrote the code. To offer a commercial license covering contributions from many people, the maintainer needs permission from each one.

The CLA grants that permission. Without it, the maintainer could only commercialise their own code, not contributions from others. That creates legal fragmentation and makes commercial licensing unworkable in practice.

The CLA does not transfer ownership. Contributors keep full copyright over their contributions. The CLA grants a license to use the code, not ownership of it. You can still publish, reuse, and independently license your own contributions however you want.

---

## What contributors do not lose

- Copyright ownership of their contributions
- The right to reuse their own code in any other project
- The right to publish or license their own contributions independently
- Any rights not explicitly granted in the CLA

---

## How commercial license revenue is used

Revenue funds ongoing development and maintenance: keeping the project active, the dependencies current, and the documentation accurate.

Flowo will remain available under AGPL-3.0 regardless of commercial revenue. The open source release is not conditional on the commercial side succeeding.
